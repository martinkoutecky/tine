# tine-store public surface, budgeted per concept (og-surface review §6 rule 2).
# Enforced by tests/shallow_ratchet.rs:
# - every production public item sits in exactly one `## Concept (ceiling: N)`
#   section, and N is that section's entry count: a cut lowers it, growth is a
#   visible edit of it;
# - the concept list is fixed in shallow_ratchet.rs; a NEW concept needs Martin;
# - raising a ceiling names, in the commit, the existing item of that concept
#   that cannot answer the need with a parameter, and why (rule 3: performance
#   work adds no surface: a cheaper answer is an internal fast path or a
#   parameter such as `InventoryScope` or `Depth`, never a sibling method);
# - each `fn`/`const`/`field` has a production caller in graph-features,
#   src-tauri or a tine-store binary, or carries `# test-oracle: <reason>` and a
#   `test-faults` gate (rule 4);
# - no public name, signature or doc promises cache, load or readiness state,
#   and nothing answers `Option<bool>` (rule 5).
# Totals (operations, WholeGraph questions, types) are derived from these
# sections and printed with TINE_SHALLOW_PRINT=1; they are not targets.
