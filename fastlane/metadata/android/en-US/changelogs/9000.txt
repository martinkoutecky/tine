Tine 0.9.0 makes the 0.9 Beta line stable. New: margin comments with replies, git built in (automatic commits, sync, cloning), a Share dialog with folder exports and encrypted quick-share links, a command line that can write, open tabs in the sidebar, an All graphs switcher, editable hover previews, and launch backups as one zip. Known issue: an agent writing through the CLI to the page you are typing in can cause a save conflict; nothing is lost (#964).
