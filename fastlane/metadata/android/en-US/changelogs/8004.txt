Fourth Tine 0.8 Beta, the stable candidate. Collapse and Expand (one level) now include the block itself. Typing in a zoomed block is faster on long pages. Sticky parents show formatted text and line up with their bullets; sidebar blocks and embed breadcrumbs show their parent blocks. A block link that starts Tine opens its block the first time. Search tab ArrowUp returns to the search box. Windows: fixes a rare "write target escapes graph root" save refusal.
