Second Tine 0.10 Beta, with about 125 fixes. Renaming a page to another page's alias, or renaming a journal day, opens a dialog that offers what Tine can carry out. Renames and merges are checked before anything is written. New share links run queries in the reader. Typographic replacements are on by default. Fixes for Android voice typing, the Windows window size, Git sync on large graphs, PDF search and Quick Capture. The Beta's .deb and .rpm now install next to stable Tine.
