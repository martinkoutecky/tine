A part of Tine that fails now says so and offers a Retry, instead of leaving the window blank. Any page or block property can be edited from the UI, and a query can be exported as a standalone site or a read-only app. Search on a large graph reuses its saved index instead of rebuilding it at every launch, and is about twice as fast. Fixes include cross-page move undo, journal-day moves, Org page properties, Android's photo picker, IME workspace names, and code-block languages.
